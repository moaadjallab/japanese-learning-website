## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage.

## 2. Cookies we use today

This website **does not use advertising, tracking or other non-essential cookies**.

When you log in, we set two **strictly necessary** cookies:

- `na_session`: keeps you signed in (secure, not readable by scripts, expires after 30 days of inactivity or when you log out);
- `na_auth`: contains only the value `1`, so pages can show "My account" instead of "Login". It contains no personal data.

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set **strictly necessary** security cookies (for example `__cf_bm`) and, on the contact page, the Cloudflare Turnstile anti-spam check may use similar technology. These are required to protect the website and do not need your consent.

## 3. Analytics

If we enable Cloudflare Web Analytics, it measures visits **without cookies** and without tracking you across websites. [Update this section if another analytics tool is used.]

## 4. In the future

When online payments open, our payment provider may set its own cookies on its checkout pages. If we ever add non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

## 5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop some features from working.
